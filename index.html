Reference Yuki's and/or Carlos's posts.
Record 45-second responses: (1) Do you agree or disagree that AI will eventually replace human researchers? Use specific reasons and examples. (2) Summarize the main argument about AI in scientific research from today's shadowing passage. Do you find the argument convincing? Why or why not?
Write 5 original sentences using: accelerate a discovery, groundbreaking research, leverage computational power, remarkably accurate, widely applicable. Bold or underline the collocation in each sentence.
