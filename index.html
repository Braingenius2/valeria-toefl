TOEFL iBT Exam Structure (2026)

The entire exam runs straight through in under 1.5 hours with no scheduled breaks.

Section Estimated Time Questions / Tasks Task Types & Description
Reading ~18-27 minutes 35-48 items 1. Complete the Words — fill in missing letters in a paragraph

2. Read in Daily Life — emails, campus notices

3. Read an Academic Passage — short academic texts
Listening ~18-27 minutes 35-45 items 1. Listen and Choose a Response — everyday responses

2. Conversations — campus dialogue

3. Announcements & Academic Talks
Speaking ~8 minutes 11 questions
(2 Task Types)		 1. Listen and Repeat — 7 prompts: copy a sentence exactly

2. Take an Interview — 4 prompts: answer a virtual researcher's questions on a daily topic
Writing ~23 minutes 12 items
(3 Task Types)		 1. Build a Sentence

2. Write an Email

3. Writing for an Academic Discussion — classroom forum simulation
Critical Notes
·
Zero Prep Time for Speaking: There are no longer 15-30 second preparation timers. Speaking tasks require spontaneous, immediate responses.
·
The "Adaptive" Feature: In Reading and Listening, the first set of questions is standard. The system adjusts the second set to be easier or harder based on how the student performed in the first stage.

Homework Assignments — Lesson 5
Record 60-second OREO responses (zero prep) to: (1) Should governments regulate AI-generated content? (2) Can individuals protect themselves from being deceived by deepfakes? (3) What personal habit has helped you most in learning English?

Write 5 sentences using: synthetic, disinformation, accountability, regulatory, transparency. Target: natural, academic register.
