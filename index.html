📋 TOEFL iBT Exam Structure

The total test time is about 2 hours, and all sections are completed on the same day.

Section Time Limit Questions / Tasks Task Description
📖 Reading 35 minutes 20 questions Read 2 academic passages and answer multiple-choice questions.
🎧 Listening 36 minutes 28 questions Listen to 3 lectures and 2 conversations, then answer multiple-choice questions.
🎤 Speaking 16 minutes 4 tasks 1 independent task (express an opinion) + 3 integrated tasks (read/listen, then speak).
✍️ Writing 29 minutes 2 tasks 1 integrated task (read/listen, then write) + 1 Writing for an Academic Discussion task.
💡 Key Takeaways
⚡

⏱
