Stage 4 · timed

Writing

AD — 10 minutes

Professor: Some universities now require every student to study a second language for at least one year. Should this be required, or should language study remain optional? Classmates: Mia says a second language is essential for a global job market; Daniel says students should choose based on their own goals.
10:00
0 words ·
  • Clear position in the first sentence
  • One concrete example or evidence
  • Reference to a classmate by name
  • Concession + rebuttal
  • 120-130 words, clean ending
Show the model answer👁️
I think universities should require one year of language study, but they should let students choose which language. The strongest argument is that a second language changes how students see their own field: a future doctor who reads Spanish-language research notices questions that English-only reading would hide. That said, I agree with Daniel that a fixed requirement can waste time, so the programme should be flexible: one year, any language, with an option to test out. In my own case, studying German made scientific vocabulary easier to break down, and it gave me the confidence to read short papers without translating every sentence. Language study is not decoration; it is a tool, and one year is enough to learn how to use it.
