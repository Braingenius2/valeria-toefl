The rise of synthetic media powered by artificial intelligence has blurred the line between reality and fabrication. Deepfake technology which can generate hyper-realistic video and audio of people saying things they never said has advanced at an alarming rate. While proponents highlight creative applications in entertainment and education, critics warn of unprecedented risks such as political disinformation, identity theft, and the erosion of public trust. As detection tools struggle to keep pace with generation techniques, the question becomes not whether we can spot fakes, but whether we will believe anything we see.
