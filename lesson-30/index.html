📚 Reading & Listening — current-format sampler (16 min)

Reading: one short item from each task family. Listening: one sample from each task type. Treat this as a diagnostic sample, not a scaled section score.

READING · COMPLETE THE WORDS

Students who take short bre___ often remem___ more. They can concen_____ for longer per_____ and feel less ti___ at the end.

breaks · remember · concentrate · periods · tired. Use the sentence meaning and word form to restore the missing letters.
READING · READ IN DAILY LIFE
Study Room Bookings: reserve at least 12 hours ahead. Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the start count as a used session. Group bookings last up to 45 minutes.

What happens if a student cancels 90 minutes before the booking?

A. The booking is moved automatically · B. It counts as used · C. The student gets a full refund

B — cancellation is less than two hours before the start.
READING · ACADEMIC PASSAGE

A campus study compared students who used short retrieval quizzes with students who reread their notes. Both groups studied for the same amount of time. One week later, the quiz group recalled more details. The researchers cautioned that the quizzes were brief and focused on key concepts; they were not a replacement for careful reading. The team concluded that retrieving information helps learners identify what they have not understood and strengthens later recall.

What is the passage's main claim? A. Rereading is always harmful · B. Brief retrieval practice can strengthen later recall · C. Students should study for longer each day

B — the passage contrasts equal-time rereading with retrieval and reports stronger later recall for the retrieval group.
