Academic Passage

For decades, schools measured reading ability by speed. A new study suggests that this measure hides an important difference: students who read quickly may skip the connections between paragraphs, while slower readers often build a clearer model of the argument. The researchers asked 400 students to read two texts and then explain the author's position. Fast readers recalled more individual facts; slower readers gave more accurate summaries. The team concludes that speed should be one measure among several, not the definition of skill.

What is the main claim?

Reading speed is the best measure of ability
Comprehension needs more than speed
Slow readers always remember more facts
Check👁️
B — The study contrasts fact recall with accurate summaries and concludes speed is only one measure.
