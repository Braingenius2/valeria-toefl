✍️ Academic Discussion — AI in Research
👨‍🏫 Professor's Prompt

"AI is transforming how research is conducted across all academic disciplines. Some argue that AI will accelerate scientific progress more than any other technology in history. Others worry that over-reliance on AI could narrow the types of questions researchers ask. What do you think?"

👩 Dr. Elena Marchetti (classmate):

"AI is undoubtedly the most transformative research tool we have ever created. In my field of drug discovery, machine learning algorithms have accelerated the identification of candidate molecules from years to months. This is groundbreaking progress that would be impossible through traditional methods alone. I believe AI will unlock discoveries we cannot even imagine yet."

👨 Prof. James Okonkwo (classmate):

"I am more cautious. When researchers rely heavily on AI, they tend to frame their questions around what the algorithms can analyze — large datasets with clear patterns. This could marginalize qualitative research and theoretical work that does not fit neatly into a computational model. We risk narrowing science rather than expanding it."
