Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing scientific research, accelerating discoveries in fields from drug development to climate modeling. Machine learning algorithms can analyze vast datasets in hours, a task that would take human researchers years to complete. In biology, AI has predicted protein structures with remarkable accuracy. In astronomy, it has identified previously unknown exoplanets. Despite these breakthroughs, researchers caution that AI systems are tools, not replacements for human intuition and creativity. The most promising approach combines computational power with human expertise, leveraging the strengths of both.
