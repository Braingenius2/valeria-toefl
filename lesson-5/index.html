🎤 TOEFL "Take an Interview" Format

Format

  • 3–5 questions asked sequentially
  • 45 seconds per response
  • Zero prep time — speak immediately
  • Topics: education, technology, society, personal experience

ETS Scoring Criteria

  • Fluency & Coherence: Sustain speech 45s without long pauses
  • Language Use: Varied vocabulary and grammar naturally
  • Topic Development: Clear opinion + reason + example
🧱 OREO Compressed Structure (45-Second Responses)
ComponentTimeContentExample
O — Opinion5sState your position clearly"I firmly believe that..."
R — Reason10sGive your primary reason"The main reason is that..."
E — Example20sProvide a specific example"For instance, in my own experience..."
O — Restated10sConclude and connect back"That's why I think..."
⏱ Time-Filling & Recovery Strategies
Acknowledge the question: "That's an interesting question. Let me think about that for a moment..."
Paraphrase before answering: "So you're asking whether... Well, I would say that..."
Buy time naturally: "I've never really thought about it that way, but my initial reaction is..."
Recovery frames: "To put it differently..." / "What I'm trying to say is..."

🎯 Drill 1: Quick Opinion — OREO Practice

15 seconds planning → 45 seconds speaking. Use the OREO structure.

Prompt A: "Do you think it's better to work for a large company or start your own business? Why?"
Model: "Personally, I believe working for a large company is a better choice for most people early in their careers. The main reason is that large companies offer structured training programs and mentorship opportunities that startups simply cannot provide. For example, when my friend graduated, she joined a multinational corporation and received six months of formal training, exposure to international teams, and a clear career progression path. Someone starting their own business would have to figure all of that out alone. That's why I think gaining experience in a larger organization first is the more practical path."
Prompt B: "Some people prefer to study alone. Others prefer group study. Which do you prefer?"
Model: "I firmly believe that studying alone is more effective for deep understanding. The main reason is that group settings often create distractions and social pressure rather than genuine learning. For instance, when I prepared for important exams, I found that studying independently allowed me to focus on my weak areas and move at my own pace. That's why I prefer solitary study for mastering complex material."
Prompt C: "Should universities focus more on teaching practical skills or theoretical knowledge?"
Model: "I think universities should strike a balance between practical skills and theoretical knowledge. The main reason is that theory provides the foundation while practice builds competence. For example, a medical student needs both anatomy textbooks and clinical rotations. Focusing only on one would leave graduates unprepared. That's why I believe an integrated approach is the best path forward."
⏱ Drill 2: Fillers & Recovery (Immediate Response)

Start speaking within 2 seconds. Use a filler strategy if needed.

Prompt D: "What is one skill every young professional should learn, and why?"
Model: "That's a great question. Let me think... I would say that every young professional should learn how to communicate effectively in English. The reason is that English has become the global language of business, regardless of which industry you work in. For instance, even in Russian companies, many technical documents and international meetings are conducted in English. Without strong English skills, you limit your career opportunities significantly. So yes, I believe English communication is the most valuable skill a young professional can develop."
Prompt E: "Do you agree that technology makes people less productive? Why or why not?"
Model: "That's an interesting question. So you're asking whether technology hurts productivity... I would disagree with that statement. The main reason is that technology automates repetitive tasks and frees up time for creative work. For instance, tools like project management software and AI writing assistants help me complete tasks that used to take hours in just minutes. However, I admit that without discipline, technology can be distracting. To put it differently, the problem isn't technology itself but how we use it."
✏️ Drill 3: Error Correction (Recycled Targets)

Correct these sentences from hypothetical speaking responses.

1. "The question is why do many people choose to study abroad."
Correct: "The question is why many people choose to study abroad." (Indirect question — no auxiliary inversion)
2. "The student argue that online learning is less effective."
Correct: "The student argues that online learning is less effective." (3rd person -s)
3. "Government should provide free education for everyone."
Correct: "The government should provide free education for everyone." (Article needed)
