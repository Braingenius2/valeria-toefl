TOEFL Speaking: Listen and Repeat Format

The Listen and Repeat task is a core component of the 2026 TOEFL Speaking section. You must:

  1. Hear a sentence once — no second play.
  2. Repeat the sentence exactly as you heard it.
  3. Score: Accuracy, pronunciation, and completeness.
  4. Length: 4–6 sentences per test, 8–25 words each.
Why it is hard for Russian speakers: Russian has predictable stress, syllable-timed rhythm, and no articles or weak forms. English has unpredictable stress, stress-timed rhythm, and frequent reduced vowels — all of which make exact repetition challenging.
Three Core Skills

1. Chunking

Group words into meaningful phrases. Each chunk has one main stress.

"The exploration of deep space // represents one of humanity's most ambitious // scientific undertakings."

2. Stress Tracking

Content words (nouns, main verbs, adjectives) are stressed. Function words (articles, prepositions) are reduced.

"SOLAR panels PROvide the PRIMARY SOURCE of POWer FOR MOST SPACE PROBES."

3. Weak Form Awareness

Function words are pronounced with reduced vowels in connected speech:

Drill 1: Chunking Boundaries

Draw // boundaries between natural stress groups. Tap to reveal.

1. "Mars missions require precise calculations and advanced engineering."
Answer: "Mars missions // require precise calculations // and advanced engineering."
2. "The surface temperature on Venus is hot enough to melt lead and zinc."
Answer: "The surface temperature on Venus // is hot enough // to melt lead and zinc."
3. "Collaboration between space agencies across the world has been essential to the success of modern space exploration."
Answer: "Collaboration between space agencies across the world // has been essential // to the success of modern space exploration."
Drill 2: Stress Tracking — Gap-fill

Clap on the stressed syllables in each sentence. Fill in the missing stressed words.

1. "Solar ______ provide the ______ source of ______ for most space ______."
Answer: "Solar panels provide the primary source of power for most space probes."
2. "______ aboard the International Space Station ______ experiments that are ______ to perform on ______."
Answer: "Astronauts aboard the International Space Station conduct experiments that are impossible to perform on Earth."

📊 Self-Evaluation Checklist

After completing each sentence, evaluate your performance honestly.

CriteriaYesPartiallyNo
1. I reproduced the complete sentence without omissions
2. My pronunciation of each word was clear and accurate
3. I placed stress on the correct syllables and content words
4. My intonation followed the natural melody of the sentence
5. I grouped words into natural chunks or phrases
6. I repeated the sentence smoothly without long pauses
