🎙️ Shadowing — Vocabulary Precision
Precise vocabulary distinguishes competent academic writing from merely adequate prose. Where vague language relies on general terms like good or important, precise word choice selects the exact term that conveys the intended meaning. Academic register demands specific lexical items: substantiate rather than prove, demonstrate rather than show, detrimental rather than bad. This precision eliminates ambiguity and signals to examiners that the writer possesses both lexical range and the judgement to deploy it effectively. Over the course of a timed response, even small vocabulary upgrades accumulate into a measurably stronger performance.

Targets: precise vocabulary · academic register · lexical range · deploy effectively · small vocabulary upgrades

🔧 Vague → Precise: The Upgrade Toolkit

Replace vague words with precise alternatives. Each upgrade improves your lexical resource score.

good→beneficial / advantageous / favourable
bad→detrimental / adverse / harmful
important→essential / crucial / paramount
show→demonstrate / illustrate / indicate
prove→substantiate / confirm / validate
get→obtain / acquire / gain
help→facilitate / assist / contribute to
more and more→an increasing number of / a growing trend

⚠️ TOEFL trap: Don't over-upgrade. Use precise vocabulary only where it fits naturally. Forced synonyms sound awkward and reduce coherence.
