🎙️ Shadowing
Prosodic variation is the primary acoustic signal that distinguishes fluent, authoritative speech from hesitant, monotone delivery. By strategically modulating pitch peaks, adjusting speaking rate, and emphasizing contrastive stress, speakers convey analytical depth and rhetorical confidence. In high-stakes interview contexts, deliberate prosodic control not only clarifies logical structure for listeners but also projects cognitive mastery over complex subject matter.
🗣️ 3-Tier Prosody Framework
Tier 1: Basic (Flat)
"Students should study more. It helps their grades."
⚠️ Robotic, low fluency score
Tier 2: Connected (Standard)
"Students should study more, because it helps their grades."
✅ Fluent, clear linking
Tier 3: Emphatic (Advanced)
STUDents should study more, because it helps their grades.
🚀 Authoritative, high scores
📢 Drill 1: Tier 1 — Single Complex Sentences (15-18 words)
Strategy: Group subject + verb into one breath chunk. Pitch: slight rise at the comma, fall at the end.

Although initial costs were high, the university successfully implemented its new digital library system across all departments.

✉️ Email Writing: Session Practice & Review
5-Part Formula (from L15): Greeting → Acknowledge → Requests (one paragraph each) → Offer/polite close. Polite request phrases: "Could you share…" · "I would really appreciate it if you could…" · "Could you please tell me…"
📧 Worked Example A: Guest Lecture Follow-up
Prompt: You attended a guest lecture by Dr. Amina Rashid on Monday about how to stay productive and manage stress as a college student. Too many students were waiting to ask a follow-up question after the talk. Write to Dr. Rashid: explain that you attended and briefly mention what you found especially helpful; ask if she could share more information about the techniques she talked about; ask if she has any suggestions for resources you could use to improve your study habits this semester.
💡 What we practiced: one paragraph per request · polite openers (Could you share… / I would really appreciate it if you could…) · concrete follow-ups (articles, books, websites).

Hello Dr. Rashid,

I attended your lecture on Monday about managing stress, and enjoyed it quite a lot. Several of my classmates also enjoyed it, so I must thank you for taking the time to visit our campus and give such an inspiring presentation. All of us found your comments about dealing with exam-related anxiety particularly useful.

If possible, could you share additional information about the strategies you mentioned during the talk? Perhaps you could pass along copies of the articles you referred to, or examples of how students have put certain strategies into practice.

Moreover, I would really appreciate it if you could recommend some resources I could utilize to improve my study habits. Are there any books you would recommend? Or perhaps there are some websites I could check out for additional ideas.

Thank you very much for your time,
Michael Schertzer

🏨 Worked Example B: Hotel Booking Inquiry
Prompt: You are planning a short holiday next month and found an advertisement for a hotel that looks suitable. However, the advertisement does not clearly say whether breakfast is included or if there is parking available. Write to the hotel owner: explain why you are interested in the hotel; ask whether breakfast and parking are included; request information about the easiest way to make a booking.
💡 What we practiced: reason first, then one question block per topic (breakfast / parking / booking) · polite closers (Could you please tell me… / Thanks for your time and attention).

Dear Hotel Manager,

I am planning a short stay in your city and came across a flyer advertising your hotel. I think I'd be interested in booking a stay in your facility because firstly, it is the only 5-star hotel in the vicinity, also, it has large swimming pools and saunas. All of which I think would make my stay memorable.

Moreover, could you give me information on whether you provide breakfast, if it's included in the price or would I have to pay separately for it. I also have a ride and would like to know if parking is available in the hotel.

Could you please tell me the easiest way to make a reservation? Maybe on the website or through the phone now?

Thanks for your time and attention to this,
Fortune

Dear Professor Martin, Thank you for inviting students to share their views on the exchange program with our partner university in Spain. I believe the main benefit is cultural immersion, since living abroad develops both language skills and adaptability, which are essential for international careers. My main concern is financial support, because not all students can afford travel and accommodation costs. As a suggestion, the university could offer scholarships or subsidized housing for participants. I would be happy to join the planning committee and help promote the program among students. Please let me know how I can contribute. Best regards, Valeria
