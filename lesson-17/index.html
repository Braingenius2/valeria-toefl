🎙️ Shadowing: Strategy Language
Composing a substantive academic response under strict temporal constraints requires deliberate structural planning. To achieve the optimal length threshold without sacrificing analytical depth or introducing redundant assertions, proficient writers employ specific rhetorical strategies: contextual framing, concession-rebuttal pairings, concrete exemplification, and precise concluding synthesis. These techniques simultaneously satisfy quantitative length requirements and demonstrate sophisticated command of academic discourse conventions.

Targets: substantive · constraints · deliberate · threshold · proficient · rhetorical · exemplification · synthesis

📐 C1 Expansion Framework — 10-Minute Academic Discussion

Official task: at least 100 words. Practice target: 120–130 words in 10 minutes (includes reading, planning, writing, checking). This 4-sentence frame builds that length without filler.

Sentence 1: Stance (18-22 words)
"While [Concession], I firmly maintain that [Your Opinion], because [Core Reason]."
Sentence 2: Development (22-28 words)
"This is primarily because [Elaboration on reason], and [Additional support], which [Impact]."
Sentence 3: Counter-argument (28-35 words)
"Critics often argue that [Opposing view], yet [Rebuttal], and [Stronger argument], demonstrating [Significance]."
Sentence 4: Synthesis (22-28 words)
"Therefore, combining [Your solution] with [Supporting element] is essential for achieving [Goal], not merely [Alternative]."
Frame ≈ 90–113 words before elaboration · With one example and precise linking you reach 120–130
How to expand without padding: add one concrete example (7–12 words) to Sentence 2 or 3, and one precise linker (for example, however, furthermore). Do not add a fifth sentence.
✍️ Sprint 1: Traditional Schools vs. Online Learning (10 min)
10:00
Task: State and support your opinion. Make a meaningful contribution using the classmates' ideas. Minimum: 100 words. Practice target: 120–130 words.

Professor: "With the rapid advancement of digital platforms, some argue that traditional brick-and-mortar schools will eventually become obsolete. Others contend that physical classrooms remain essential for student development. What is your position?"

Alex: "Online learning is much more flexible and cheaper than traditional schools."
Emma: "Traditional schools develop social skills through face-to-face interaction. This is vital for children."
Word count: 0 words (Minimum: 100; target: 120-130)
Model Answer (Band 4+):

While I acknowledge Emma's concern that physical classrooms develop essential social skills through face-to-face interaction, I firmly maintain that traditional schools will remain essential, because they provide irreplaceable holistic development that digital platforms cannot fully replicate. This is primarily because structured in-person environments foster spontaneous peer collaboration, which builds communication competencies that transcend academic content, and these interpersonal skills directly impact long-term career readiness. Critics often argue that online learning's flexibility and affordability render physical campuses obsolete, yet this perspective overlooks the fact that remote students frequently report isolation and diminished motivation, demonstrating that educational effectiveness requires social infrastructure beyond mere content delivery. Therefore, combining structured digital resources with the physical school environment is essential for achieving comprehensive student development, not merely efficient content consumption.

✍️ Sprint 2: Artificial Intelligence in Education (10 min)
10:00
Task: State and support your opinion. Make a meaningful contribution using the classmates' ideas. Minimum: 100 words. Practice target: 120–130 words.

Professor: "AI-powered tutoring systems are becoming increasingly sophisticated. Some believe these systems will eventually replace human teachers. Others argue that human teachers are irreplaceable. What is your view?"

Mark: "AI tutors are always available, which is great for students in different time zones."
Lisa: "Human teachers provide emotional support and mentorship that AI cannot replicate."
Word count: 0 words (Minimum: 100; target: 120-130)
Model Answer (Band 4+):

While I acknowledge Mark's point that AI tutors offer unprecedented accessibility across time zones, I firmly maintain that human teachers are irreplaceable, because they provide emotional intelligence and adaptive mentorship that no algorithm can authentically replicate. This is primarily because effective education requires reading non-verbal cues, adjusting pedagogical approaches in real-time, and building trust-based relationships, and these deeply human capacities directly determine student engagement and long-term academic persistence. Critics often argue that AI's data-processing superiority and 24/7 availability make human instruction obsolete, yet this perspective ignores the fact that learning is fundamentally a social process, demonstrating that technological efficiency alone cannot substitute for empathetic human connection. Therefore, integrating AI tools as supplements within a human-centered teaching framework is essential for achieving both personalized instruction and meaningful student-teacher mentorship.

✉️ Write an Email (7 min, 100-120 words)
07:00
Email from Coach Rivera: "The campus gym must increase its membership fees next semester to pay for new equipment. I would like your opinion on this change and one suggestion for keeping membership affordable."

In your email:
  1. Say whether you support the fee increase and explain why.
  2. Suggest one idea for keeping membership affordable for students.
  3. Offer to help communicate the change or collect student opinions.
Word count: 0 words (Minimum: 100; target: 100-120)
Model Answer (Band 4+):

Dear Coach Rivera,

Thank you for explaining the planned increase in gym membership fees. I understand the need to pay for new equipment and I support the change if it improves safety and training options for students.

One idea for keeping membership affordable would be to offer a discounted semester pass for students who register early. This would let students budget in advance while guaranteeing steady membership numbers for the gym.

I would be happy to help collect student opinions about the new schedule or share the updated information with classmates. Please let me know if you would like me to circulate a short survey before the new term begins.

Best regards,
Valeria

📚 Homework: Write an Email — Internship Recommendation

Official format: 7 minutes, 100–120 words. Address all three bullets in a polite, formal email to a professor.

✉️ Homework — Email to Professor Anderson (7 min, 100–120 words)
07:00
Scenario: You are applying for a summer internship at a local company. The application requires a letter of recommendation from a professor who knows your academic work well. You took Professor Anderson's computer science course last semester and earned a high grade.

Write an email to Professor Anderson. In your email, do the following:
  1. Explain why you are applying for the summer research program.
  2. Ask Professor Anderson to write a letter of recommendation for you.
  3. Offer to provide any information that would help Professor Anderson write the letter.
Word count: 0 words (Minimum: 100; target: 100-120)
Model Answer — Human, formal, 118 words:

Dear Professor Anderson,

I hope you are well. I am writing to ask if you would be willing to write a letter of recommendation for my application to a summer research internship at NovaTech, a local software company. I am applying because the program focuses on machine learning for educational tools, which directly connects to the final project I completed in your Computer Science course last semester.

I earned an A in your course and particularly valued your feedback on my algorithm design, so I thought you could speak well to my analytical skills and work ethic.

I would be happy to provide my CV, transcript, the internship description, and a brief summary of my course project to make writing the letter easier. The deadline is May 10, and I can send all materials this week. Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,
Valeria
