## 2. Input & Strategy (15 min)

### A. TOEFL Reading Recovery: Dissecting Inference Questions
- **The "Outside Knowledge Trap":** Inference questions (*"What can be inferred from paragraph X?"*) ask for logical implications strictly supported by the text. Incorrect distractor options often state real-world facts that are *not* directly backed by the passage.
- **The Golden Rule of TOEFL Inference:** The correct answer is always a direct logical extension of 1-2 specific sentences in the text.
- **3-Step Inference Method:**
  1. Locate keywords in the prompt.
  2. Bracket the target sentence + 1 surrounding sentence.
  3. Rephrase: *"If [Text] is true, then [Option] MUST be true without adding outside facts."*

### B. TOEFL Writing Recovery: The 8-Minute Discussion Progression
- **Target:** 120–140 words in 8 minutes (compressing the standard 10-minute limit for exam-day buffer).
- **The 5-Sentence Structural Formula:**
  1. **Position & Concession:** *"While I acknowledge [Peer's Name]'s point that [X], I strongly maintain that [Your Position]."*
  2. **Core Rationale:** *"This is primarily because [Key Reason], which directly impacts [Context]."*
  3. **Counter-Argument Anchor:** *"Critics often argue that [Counterpoint], yet this perspective overlooks the fact that [Rebuttal]."*
  4. **Concrete Evidence/Illustration:** *"For instance, in recent studies regarding [Topic], researchers observed that [Specific Example]."*
  5. **Synthesis & Conclusion:** *"Therefore, balancing [Factor A] with [Factor B] is essential for achieving long-term [Outcome]."*

### C. Predictive Error Targets (Anticipated Russian Transfer & Concession Traps)
| Error Category | Incorrect Structure | Correct TOEFL Upgrade | Rule / Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Double Concession** | *"Although it is expensive, but it is necessary."* | *"Although it is expensive, it is necessary."* | Never pair *although/even though* with *but/however* in the same clause. |
| **Preposition Transfer** | *"It depends from the government policy."* | *"It depends on government policy."* | *Depend* takes *on/upon*, never *from*. |
| **Word Order in Subordination** | *"I agree with Liam why should we regulate AI."* | *"I agree with Liam regarding why we should regulate AI."* | Statement word order in noun clauses (subject before verb). |
| **Conjunction Misuse** | *"Despite the technology is advancing..."* | *"Despite the rapid advancement of technology..."* OR *"Although technology is advancing..."* | *Despite* requires a noun phrase; *although* requires a full clause. |

## 3. Controlled Practice (10 min)

### Exercise 1: Inference Discrimination (Select True Inference vs. Outside Knowledge Trap)

**Passage Segment:**
> "The implementation of automated grading systems in higher education has significantly reduced turnaround time for student assessment. However, pedagogical experts emphasize that while algorithms excel at evaluating multiple-choice and standardized computational problems, they struggle to evaluate qualitative arguments, nuanced critical thinking, and stylistic creativity in humanities essays."

1. **Question:** What can be inferred about automated grading systems from the passage?
   - (A) Automated grading systems will eventually replace human professors in humanities departments.
   - (B) Automated grading is more suitable for STEM fields than for qualitative humanities evaluations.
   - (C) Students prefer receiving quick feedback from automated systems rather than waiting for professor comments.
   - (D) Humanities essays cannot be graded fairly by any existing computational tool.
   - **Answer:** **(B)**
   - **Explanation:** The text states algorithms excel at computational problems but struggle with humanities essays, making them logically more suitable for STEM/quantitative tasks. (A) and (C) introduce unstated outside assumptions. (D) uses absolute language ("cannot be graded fairly by any") which exceeds the text's claim.

### Exercise 2: Concession & Subordination Correction
Correct the following sentences:
1. *"Despite higher education is costly, but many students pursue degrees abroad."*
   - **Key:** *"Although higher education is costly, many students pursue degrees abroad."* (or *"Despite the high cost of higher education, many students..."*)
2. *"The study shows why are inference questions difficult for students."*
   - **Key:** *"The study shows why inference questions are difficult for students."*
3. *"The outcome depends from how well students manage their time during the exam."*
   - **Key:** *"The outcome depends on how well students manage their time during the exam."*
